Own a boat?

It can become tours and income, without you handling listings, customer service or payments.

Bordazul was born from a boat tour company in Búzios that already does this every day: it welcomes the client, answers questions, confirms the date and collects the deposit. The owner only takes care of the boat and the tour.

Now we are taking this way of working to other seas in Brazil. Sign-ups open step by step, one destination at a time, so each city starts with good boats.

If you own a motorboat, yacht, sailboat, catamaran or schooner with a licensed skipper, talk to us. You set the price you want to receive, and the boat is on the calendar only when you want.

What is Bordazul?+

A platform to rent boats with a skipper in Brazil's nautical destinations, with boats from many owners in one place.

Where are boats available now?+

In Búzios, Rio de Janeiro. There you can see every boat, photos and seasonal prices and book online. Other destinations open step by step.

Does every boat come with a skipper?+

Yes. Every tour has a licensed skipper, and the boat is for your group only.

What is included in Búzios?+

Fuel, ice, charcoal, mineral water and a cooler. Food and drinks are not included, and the crew grills for you if you bring the meat.

How do I book?+

Choose the boat, the date and the number of people. The date is secured with a 50% deposit and the rest is paid on the day of the tour.

How can I pay?+

The deposit can be paid by Pix for any date, and by card when the tour is 5 or more days away.

What if I need to cancel?+

You can keep a credit of 100% of the deposit for 1 year. With 7 or more days' notice, you can also ask for the full deposit back.

What if the sea is rough?+

The tour is moved to another date at no cost.

When will my city open?+

As soon as it has good boats to rent. Ask on WhatsApp to be told first.

Can I list my boat?+

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp: sign-ups are opening destination by destination.

Who is behind Bordazul?+

Búzios Lanchas, a registered company that already takes groups on boat tours in Búzios.

Off to Búzios?

Boats from 22 to 35 feet, for up to 16 people, on 4 or 7 hour tours. The first fleet is already on the water. The rest of Brazil is right behind.
