Frequently asked questions

Where does RJ Lanchas have boats today?

Today our tours are in Búzios, with the Búzios Lanchas fleet. You can see every boat, photos and prices at lanchaembuzios.com.br. Other destinations in the state will show up here as soon as they have boats.

What is included in the boat rental?

Fuel, ice, charcoal, mineral water and a cooler. Food and drinks are not included, and the crew grills for you if you bring the meat.

How many people fit on the boat?

It depends on the boat. Miss Bardot, our main boat in Búzios, is 35 feet and takes up to 16 people, with air conditioning, a generator and an air fryer. Midas is 32 feet and takes up to 12.

How long is the tour?

The most popular tours are 4 hours and 7 hours. Other times can be arranged on WhatsApp.

How do I book?

Choose your boat on the Búzios website or message us on WhatsApp +55 21 99927-2550. Your booking is confirmed with a deposit and the rest is paid at boarding.
